A QUICK TOUR

Paint the atmosphere.

1. Open a map at its original export size.

2. Add an effect, then paint on the map. Use Fill map for a whole-scene effect. Show painted area reveals its mask.

3. Adjust intensity, scale, speed, and colour. Add layers to combine effects. Drag layers in the left list to reorder them, or use the arrow buttons. The bottom layer is applied first. Use × to delete a layer.

4. Save a project to keep your map, effect layers, masks, and settings together. Reopen it to keep editing.

5. Export WebM. The file is also saved in the program’s Exports folder. Enable repeat playback in your tabletop.

B Paint · E Erase · H Pan · Space + drag to pan · Ctrl+Z Undo stroke · Scroll to zoom · Hold “original” to compare.

Heat shimmer and water motion deliberately bend pixels inside the painted area. Paint carefully around grid lines if you want them to remain straight. All effects repeat smoothly at your chosen loop length.
