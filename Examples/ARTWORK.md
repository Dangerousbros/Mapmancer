# Example artwork

The three examples were supplied by the project's map author for inclusion in this free release. They contain flattened Dungeondraft map exports and editable Mapmancer animation masks.

Maps use artwork from [Crosshead Studios](https://crossheadstudios.com/dungeondraft/) and Dungeondraft. Original asset copyrights remain with their respective creators. No raw asset packs, extracted textures or Dungeondraft map source files are included.

The application MIT licence does not grant rights to extract, sell, relicense or redistribute the underlying third-party artwork. The examples are supplied as demonstrations and for tabletop play. For uses beyond the rights you already hold, obtain permission from the relevant artwork creators. Screenshots depicting these examples have the same artwork exclusion.
